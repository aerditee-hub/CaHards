How to Play
⚔Each duelist is dealt 9 cards — three Magic, three Tech, three Strength — shuffled at the start.
✦Every round, both sides secretly choose one card. They're revealed together.
▸Magic defeats Tech. Tech defeats Strength. Strength defeats Magic.
✚The winner adds both cards to their own hand — fully playable again.
↺A tie (same type) voids the round — each card returns to its original owner.
♛The duel ends the moment a player runs out of cards. Their rival claims victory.
